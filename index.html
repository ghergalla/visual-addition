Why does a carried dot change size and color?

Settled droplets stay still while incoming dots follow curved paths into the open positions. Only a group of ten flows together into a circle with ten times the area of one dot. As it travels left, that bundle becomes one dot in the next place: 10 ones = 1 ten, 10 tens = 1 hundred, and so on. The amount stays the same. An empty place becomes a zero.
